## What This Project Is

The Velvet Core Job Tracker is not a generic spreadsheet template. It is a working job-search command system created to reduce the friction of finding, evaluating, prioritizing, and tracking opportunities.

The system combines job-search organization, company targeting, role filtering, application tracking, follow-up planning, and geographic strategy in one workbook.

## Current Workbook Structure

The workbook currently includes:

- **Weekly Planner** — organizes job-search activity and weekly execution
- **Job Log** — records opportunities, applications, status, and follow-up activity
- **Data Technician Searches** — stores focused search results for technical and data-adjacent roles
- **Operation Alpharetta** — prioritizes companies and roles around Alpharetta, with Roswell and Sandy Springs as backup zones
- **100% WFH - Atlanta Metro** — tracks fully remote opportunities connected to the Atlanta market

## Operation Alpharetta

Operation Alpharetta is a targeted company pipeline built around commute reality, salary goals, career fit, and role strategy.

## The Problem It Solves

Job searching becomes inefficient when opportunities are scattered across tabs, saved posts, email, job boards, notes, and memory.

This system solves that by creating one operational view for:

- discovering opportunities
- ranking companies
- matching roles to experience
- avoiding duplicate work
- tracking application progress
- planning follow-ups
- organizing resume versions
- managing location and commute priorities
- maintaining salary discipline

## AI-Assisted Development and Vibe Coding

The workflow was shaped through sustained human direction and AI-assisted iteration.

The process included:

1. Define the real job-search problem.
2. Identify the decisions that needed to be made repeatedly.
3. Translate those decisions into workbook structure, columns, tabs, filters, and status fields.
4. Use AI to help organize company research, role categories, search logic, and workflow language.
5. Test the workbook against real job opportunities.
6. revise weak fields, unclear categories, and missing tracking steps.
7. Expand the workbook as new search strategies emerged.
8. Keep final judgment, prioritization, and application decisions human-led.

This is the kind of vibe coding that combines natural-language planning, AI-supported structuring, and repeated operational testing rather than producing a one-time generated template.
